## Set-up

* Task: hide a stretch of ball that IS tracked (1, 2, 5 or 10 s), with at least 2 s of tracked ball on
  each side, and reconstruct it from the players and the surrounding ball. Score against what was
  hidden. Same trials for every method.
* Time-ordered split (minutes counted from the first graded frame of a half; 1 minute buffers
  between periods are unused): training = half 1 + half 2 minutes 0-20 (73.0 min); validation = half 2
  minutes 21-31 (10.0 min); test = half 2 minutes 32-end (20.9 min). Teams swap ends at half-time, so
  the training data is mirrored at random (horizontal and vertical flips).
* Trials: validation 187, test 267.
* Model: Set Transformer + Bi-LSTM; trained on 30 s windows at 5 Hz with randomly hidden stretches (1-25 s, or the
  whole ball hidden in 25% of windows). The loss is on hidden stretches where the truth is tracked,
  plus an auxiliary possession loss on algorithmic pseudo-labels (nearest player within 3 m of the
  tracked ball). Learning rate 0.0005, batch 16, 40 batches per epoch, possession weight 1.0.
  Best epoch 25 of 25 by validation error (9.20 m).
* Metrics: position error = mean distance in metres over the hidden steps. Team agreement = share of
  hidden steps where the team of the tracked player nearest to the guess equals that of the player
  nearest to the true ball. This is a proxy built from the tracking, NOT a possession ground truth.
